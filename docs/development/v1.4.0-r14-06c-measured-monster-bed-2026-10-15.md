## Explicit material limits

This first slice implements an **opaque base-factor × linear COLOR_0**
path, perspective-correct normal/position interpolation, direct
three-light Cook–Torrance approximation, and IEC sRGB transfer.
Actual Monster Bed has no textures, animations, normal maps,
alpha-mode blending or required glTF extensions; all its materials
are doubleSided and its fur COLOR_0 is preserved.

The worker **refuses** glTF textures/samplers, metallic-roughness maps,
normal maps, occlusion, nonzero emissive, unsupported extensions,
MASK/BLEND and nonopaque vertex-alpha. It is therefore not a full
PBR engine. In particular, IBL, shadows and many glTF material
extensions are absent. The report explicitly states
`claimScope=material-preview-only` and `certificationEligible=false`.
The provisional camera is not registered to a source photograph.
