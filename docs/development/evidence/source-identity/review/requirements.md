# Anonymous source comparison

Compare each asset's complete `source.png` against anonymous candidates A and B.
The two candidates in an asset use matched cameras, lights, renderer, resolution
and sample count. The source-sheet cameras are not registered to those renders.
Images are actual Blender renders; the paired boards only place the unchanged
render pixels beside each other with labels. Full individual images are under
`A/` and `B/`. Read whole views and relevant detail views before deciding.

## Jacket requirements

A credible editable garment prototype: cropped fitted body, hollow open front,
notched lapels and standing back collar, long shaped sleeves with open cuffs,
narrow pale piping, shoulder tabs, two front waist buckles and the rear belt.
Silver shield/chain decoration belongs on the wearer's left. Preserve the
visible silhouette, side depth and coherent garment construction; cloth should
read as cloth rather than disconnected primitive placeholders. Near-black
fabric, pale piping and metal hardware are the intended material families.

The source front/back/labelled-left views decide the decorated side; oblique
views are sometimes inconsistent. This task permits one coherent interpretation.
No body/mannequin, rig, cloth simulation, production UV layout or engine
integration is required for this prototype judgment. Exact dimensions and
connectivity cannot be certified from these images alone.

## Arm requirements

A complete cybernetic arm: shoulder socket, shaped upper-arm armor, exposed
actuator core, elbow, tapered forearm armor with a service panel, wrist and five
separate articulated digits. Preserve the ivory/graphite/gold design and broad
source proportions before small hardware. Interpret nested shells, recesses,
hand construction and mechanics from the complete source. Hidden construction
may be inferred consistently. No body or mannequin belongs to this asset.

`service-open` is a matched inspection pose for the removable cover, not a claim
that the source contains a tested working hinge. The brief separately requires
rigid pivot hierarchy and local editing; those technical properties cannot be
proven from neutral render images alone. Skinning, dynamic hydraulics, continuous
collision clearance and physical performance are not required for this visual
prototype assessment.

## Report requested

For each asset, provide (1) A/B/tie relative preference with image-specific
reasons, (2) a concise table of declared source identity and major construction
requirements marked met/partial/missing/uncertain for each candidate, (3) any
major source/construction blocker with a specific visible observation and image,
and (4) a separate finished-art acceptance judgment.

The acceptance criterion being evaluated is: “Recipe application preserves the
source-specific identity and major construction requirements declared by the
task brief.” Report whether each candidate meets that visual scope, or what
named gap prevents it. Relative preference alone does not establish acceptance.
Do not require universal finished-art quality to demonstrate the narrower
prototype criterion; equally, do not excuse a major source construction mismatch
as polish. Do not assign numerical likeness scores or infer geometry correctness
from a technical PASS. State uncertainties caused by the available views.
