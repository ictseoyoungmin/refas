## Interpretation and limit

- Reproducibility, **matching geometry and bitwise raster identity**, and modest latency differences are established for these fixtures. No functional geometry regression was detected by this matrix.
- The observed 100k candidate median is 3–7% slower in two three-repeat pairs; this might be scheduler variance or overhead. Do not silently claim an optimization or proven stable performance regression. 500k/1M ratios cluster around ~1.
- Different runs used different CPU models. **Only within-run old→candidate comparisons are comparable.** Do not compare an Intel candidate median to an AMD baseline to infer a change.
- Cold startup, original private Monster Bed full-asset new-version benchmark, Blender spectral/BRDF equivalence, registered reference photos, and third-party agent production quality are outside this test and remain unknown.
- Candidate package version 1.4.1 is metadata-only beyond tested runtime changes; release publication still needs the exact candidate Full QA, saved-v1.2.1 project upgrade, and exact postmerge main SHA gates.
