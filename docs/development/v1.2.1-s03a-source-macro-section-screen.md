## Admission and limits

Existing real-source `ensureEarlyResemblanceAdmission` already replays an immutable shape-stage actual GLB, verifies selected source-cited camera and all macro point projection fits, and screens whole-object projected bounding extents. A head, torso or other named macro part can be grossly enlarged while its central point and total object bounding box remain normal.

S03a introduces a **contradiction-only segment-envelope check** for any upstream explicitly declared source-visible macro segment in `refas.reference-geometry/v1`. Only the already replayed `refas.realized-projection/v1` (GLB POSITION geometry, node world matrices and source-selected camera) may supply projected hulls. The check rejects:
- Mismatched original-source SHA/scope or absent source-photo citation.
- Declared source-visible macro part missing from exact current GLB segmentation.
- Grossly shifted or enlarged/shrunk projected macro extents while anchor locations remain correct.
- Explicitly unresolved, inferred or occluded macro region treated as source-observed evidence.

If a source has **no declared macro segments**, prior admission stays in force: S03a does not invent a head/torso nor infer a complete source-part list. S03b will add mandatory source-observable part obligations and original-photo-driven selection. A picture that cannot expose the back/face orientation remains INSUFFICIENT. Geometry-based bounding region agreement **does not** authorize photographic likeness, 3D identity, or a one-view IoU PASS. Camera placement is never interpreted as shape truth.

This is a cheap deterministic pre-detail check, not an extra screenshot renderer or a second certification engine. Deep external photo/current GLB dogfood, head-front-vs-three-quarter orientation, shoulder occlusion ordering, body section geometry and typed upstream reopen remain S03b/S03c/S04 and external #235 acceptance.

## Tests and gate
Unit positive: independently source-annotated two-part silhouette vs real GLB mesh. Negative: enlarge a real GLB upper part while retaining its center anchor and signed projection consistency; forbid downstream detail when early gate sees this. Ambiguous inferred region must not become a false positive. Full Node20/22/24 regression, fresh-worker, VC08 on the tiered fast lane; full deep QA before final P0 closure or any release. Main remains v1.2.0.
