## Failure mechanism

A face, head, or long torso region can be turned from one diagonal orientation to its opposite while preserving center landmarks and its axis-aligned bounding box. S03a compares outer envelopes, so a re-signed but actual-GLB-derived wrong long-axis orientation must be vetoed even though all earlier center and size checks agree.

## Implementation
- Existing `ensureEarlyResemblanceAdmission` at nonfixture Host pre-detail checkpoint already decodes original SHA-bound source, freezes the source-selected camera, and verifies current shape GLB/candidate CAS and replayed `realized-projection`.
- `screenSourceMacroPartOrientation` consumes original-source-cited 2D macro segment polygons and GLB-derived projected hulls for the same source SHA/scope. Computes area-weighted polygon covariance (independent of mesh tessellation), principal *unsigned* long axis and anisotropy. A source polygon requires anisotropy >=4 to provide a reliable long-axis claim. Compared current GLB anisotropy <2 or axis mismatch >25 degrees yields REWORK. Missing original citations or GLB segment yields INSUFFICIENT.
- Square/symmetric observations do not contain a reliable long axis; they are classified `measurable:false`, **never claimed as verified face gaze**. Partly occluded/inferred source regions are controlled by S03a and cannot upgrade confidence.
- Current Host `surface-topology` and downstream admissions run this after S03a. The gate never grants positive likeness, assumes no inferred 3D depth from a single image, cannot establish head-front/back or correct facial expression. No new public certificate or single-view IoU PASS.
