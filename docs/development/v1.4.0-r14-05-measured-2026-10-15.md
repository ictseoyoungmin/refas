## Validated claim and invalid inferences

**Supported:** The latest diagnostic CPU worker successfully processed
1M *actual nondegenerate* strand triangles and materialized all three Hero
passes within 8.419 seconds / under 774 MiB observed RSS in this single
documented environment. 100k and 500k cases also completed.

**Not supported:** Any claim of scaling speedup from 100k through 1M,
performance vs legacy v1.3.0, cross-platform stable throughput, certified
multiview rendering, full material/PBR fidelity, proxy correctness,
source-resemblance improvement or real Monster Bed acceptance.
The three sizes were assigned **different CPU models** and all measurements
include cold JIT. A 1M *single repeat* is not sufficient for a production SLA.
