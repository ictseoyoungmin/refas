# QA-02 — Realized contact and support replay

The `assembly` owner provides an exact-source, exact-GLB and exact-artifact-bound replay of existing `refas.realized-contact-*/v1` contracts. In source-bound QA, the current certification checkpoint must bind exactly one each of `attachment-semantics`, `realized-contact-plan`, `realized-contact-graph`, and `realized-contact-report`. Their actual bytes, sizes, digests, and project-root containment are verified before running `replayRealizedContactEvidence`.

The replay reuses `validateRealizedContactResult`, which recomputes triangle-surface contact, unexpected penetration, and support-root reachability against the exact GLB candidate. It additionally requires every realized physical mesh to be represented in the semantic entity inventory (with digest-bound fusion aliases when appropriate), every semantic entity to resolve to actual geometry, every physical entity to be explicitly declared a support root or support-required participant, and declared roots to be FREE.

Passing this gate **does not** prove source-observed attachments are correct. A worker might still label the wrong object as a FREE root or omit a source-visible part entirely. QA-03 must compare with independent source observations, not worker-authored plan assertions. Triangle components within a mesh can be intentionally disconnected (spokes, lattices); this replay does not declare them physically unsupported merely because they are disconnected. QA-02b provides a typed index-edge component inventory, and QA-02d independently distinguishes exact geometric edge connectivity across UV/material seams; independently authorized intentional split-component exceptions remain future work.

`NOT_RUN` means required persisted evidence is absent. `INSUFFICIENT` means required entity/support evidence is incomplete or component geometry remains ambiguous. `FAIL` means required exact-byte or re-computation contracts contradict evidence. This gate must not issue or mutate a RefAs certificate.

## QA-02b — internal triangle component inventory

### QA-02d — seam-safe geometric continuity (2026-10-09)

The existing per-primitive `componentCount` remains the **index-edge**
diagnostic. It must not decide support eligibility on its own: glTF exporters
may duplicate vertices at UV/material seams or encode an entirely non-indexed
triangle soup. The new `node.spatial` inventory treats *exactly coincident
Float32 POSITION edges with opposing winding* across all primitives of a
physical node as a single geometrically contiguous shell; there is no fuzzy
epsilon that would bridge a small real gap. Same-winding overlap and
more-than-two faces incident to an edge remain independently reported as
ambiguous and require `INSUFFICIENT` rather than automatic PASS.

QA contact replay therefore checks `spatial.componentCount` and
`spatial.ambiguousEdges`, not the legacy index-edge component count.
Connected triangle soup, UV-split and multi-material representations of the
same shell are not rejected only due to exporter topology. Intentionally
separate shard geometry continues to block. Exactly coincident geometry edges
still do **not** attest manufacturing weld, physical attachment or source
resemblance, and independently source-observed QA-03 remains required.
At the QA-02d baseline, sparse POSITION accessors failed closed. QA-02l
below adds narrowly tested sparse static geometry decoding; this does not
claim general glTF attribute, morph or skinning coverage.



`inventoryGlbTriangleComponents(glb)` performs exact GLB/index access replay and
returns a SHA-bound, deterministic, per-mesh, per-primitive inventory of
**index-edge connected** triangle islands. This is *not* proof of physical
support: UV splits, spokes, meshes split across primitives and woven lattice
structures may be purposefully disconnected or overcounted.

If contact/support replay succeeds at node level but a physical mesh contains
multiple **geometrically separated** or nonmanifold/ambiguous islands, `replayRealizedContactEvidence` now returns
`INSUFFICIENT` with the inventory digest and affected physical IDs. It does
**not** claim `FAIL` for an intentional split; it also must not
return `PASS` for a floating island merely because its parent node is rooted.

Remaining QA-02 authority work must provide independent, typed, exact-byte
component support/intent evidence and a positive multi-island case (e.g., wheel
spokes, gridded basket) before allowing legitimate islands to PASS. An agent's
self-labeled `intentional` metadata cannot independently authorize the
exemption. This release slice is intentionally fail-closed.

## QA-02c — persisted propagation solver replay (dependency-free mode)

When `realized-contact-plan.propagationReportDigest` is bound, current
checkpoint artifacts **must** include one exact
`attachment-propagation-plan` and one `attachment-propagation-report`;
the loader verifies project-root containment, real source bytes and byte
size/SHA-256 before passing them into contact replay.

For plans that use only external-frame modes (`FREE`, `FUSED`,
`SUPPORTED_CLEARANCE`), the trusted `validateAttachmentPropagationReport`
**recomputes the deterministic propagation solver** from current semantic
attachments, stored plan and initial frame state. A self-authored or merely
digest-matching report cannot authorize propagation. It must be
`READY_FOR_REALIZATION`. A result is not independent source-fidelity
authorization; independent semantic/source observation belongs to QA-03.

Plans requiring surface anchors, follow-state, multi-anchor or articulated
dependencies are deliberately `INSUFFICIENT` until their entire input graph
can be read and independently replayed from current source-bound checkpoints.
Never substitute a forged or missing dependency with a signed result.
Fusion artifacts remain a separate QA-02 follow-up.

## QA-02e — exact native-fusion GLB replay

An agent-authored `physical-fusion-report` and `fusion-provenance`, even
with valid SHA digests, are **not sufficient** for source-bound support PASS.
`replayExactGlbPhysicalFusion` reconstructs the native
`WELD_SHARED_BOUNDARY` output from the **actual checkpoint-bound pre-fusion
GLB** and compares the recomputed report/provenance and Float32 geometry
against the **actual final GLB**. The pre-fusion checkpoint must have a
canonical content digest matching the bound plan and an exact input-asset
SHA reference. The final GLB must bind both reproduced report digests in
`extras.refas`. The contact replay entrypoint cannot promote fusion based
solely on caller-supplied report/provenance.

Current supported proof scope is one indexed TRIANGLES primitive per node
with identity transforms and deterministic native fusion. Complex hierarchy,
material-split source nodes or external `SOLID_UNION` backends are explicitly
`INSUFFICIENT`, not an implied PASS. Pre-fusion checkpoint project-lineage
and exact artifact-file loading are additionally owned by the source-bound
QA coverage adapter; without them, this standalone helper cannot serve as
final source-fidelity certification.

## QA-02f — independently re-evaluated propagation vs GLB world transforms

For every solver-ready, dependency-free, plan-bound propagation report, the
source-bound contact replay also resolves actual **active-scene GLB node
transforms**, including reachable parents, and compares all resolved entity
world-frame origins/axes to independently re-evaluated propagation results.
An agent can re-sign a contact plan and GLB support report after translating
or rotating a part, but cannot obtain contact PASS if its physical world pose
disagrees with the referenced attachment solver. Missing entities, duplicate
semantic node IDs or non-rigid GLB transforms are `INSUFFICIENT`;
contradicting rigid poses are `FAIL`. Tests include a re-signed translated
GLB contradiction, matching non-zero translation/rotation and non-rigid scale.

A correct realized pose does **not** demonstrate that it was observed in
the original photograph; independent source semantics (#271) remain required.
This slice does not replay surface-anchor, follow, multi-anchor and articulated
solver dependencies; those remain fail closed.

## QA-02g — dependency-complete deterministic propagation replay

The source-bound verifier now loads the exact current checkpoint artifacts
`surface-anchor-set`, `surface-descriptors`, `attachment-follow-state`,
`multi-anchor-plans`, and `articulated-joints` **only as mandated by the
propagation plan**. Each required artifact must have exactly one reference and
match persisted SHA256, byte size and project-contained path. Multi-anchor and
joint files contain arrays; the upstream deterministic planner demands exact
relation coverage and validates every individual typed plan/joint.

`replayTrustedPropagationDependencies` recreates the **propagation plan** from
those complete typed inputs, then independently regenerates the **propagation
report** via the actual DAG/multi-anchor/follow/surface/articulation solvers.
Absent dependencies are `INSUFFICIENT`; contradictory or forged inputs
are `FAIL`. The existing QA-02f active-scene GLB frame replay remains
mandatory after this solver replay, as do independent triangle contact and
source-bound provenance checks.

Important limits: a properly recomputed surface descriptor can still describe
an incorrect observed surface if source-independent geometry correlation is
absent. This check does **not** establish the source image's truth or alone
authorize final resemblance. Missing propagation applicability when no report
is bound, physical GLB frame aliases for fused semantic entities, and
geometrically exceptional disconnected components remain separately open.

## QA-02h — canonical attachment-driven propagation applicability

A contact plan's `propagationReportDigest: null` is **not** an authority
that the realized assembly has no propagation obligations. The trusted
contact verifier first validates the exact `attachment-semantics` and contact
plan, then derives applicability from the semantic DAG, independent of a
worker's contact report. This intentionally requires propagation for:

- `SURFACE_OFFSET`, `MULTI_ANCHOR`, `ARTICULATED`, or
  `SUPPORTED_CLEARANCE` relations (these require their typed solvers), and
- any non-`FREE` child relation whose owner is itself the subject of another
  non-`FREE` relation, including nested `RIGID_FOLLOW` and fused chains.

If these relations exist but the exact contact plan omits its bound
propagation-report digest, the trusted result is `INSUFFICIENT` with explicit
relation IDs. The current checkpoint's propagation plan/report files also
cannot be detached silently by clearing that digest: source-bound QA refuses
unbound files. Where propagation is required and bound, QA-02g still demands
all actual typed dependencies and deterministic solver/world-frame replay.

A single direct `RIGID_FOLLOW` edge with actual GLB triangle contact/support
proof is a distinct allowed positive: no extra external pose-solver claim is
inferred from physical proximity. A `FREE` root alone also does not acquire
an invented solver obligation. Source-independent verification of whether the
worker chose the *correct* attachment graph, including omission or relabeling
of source-visible relations, remains QA-03. This slice does not certify source
resemblance, complex fusion, or Game Ready status.

## QA-02i — realized owner-local surface descriptor proof

A typed surface-anchor set may be digest-valid and a deterministic solver may
recompute successfully while the supplying worker's `surface-descriptors`
vertices/triangles are **not** triangles in the actual candidate GLB. The
source-bound QA replay now independently parses current active-scene GLB bytes
and verifies every typed descriptor triangle against the corresponding physical
owner mesh in **owner-local coordinates**, including exact Float32 positions and
oriented winding. It accepts cyclic permutations, indexed/nonindexed triangles,
and multiple mesh primitives; it never replaces a triangle with an inferred
nearest face or a tolerance-based proxy.

- An absent, duplicate or unidentifiable owner, unsupported quantized
  accessor, skinned/morphed geometry, or untestable active scene produces
  `INSUFFICIENT` (unsupported formats need independent evidence, not PASS).
  QA-02l additionally supports rigorously decoded sparse FLOAT32 positions
  and unsigned indices, but does not relax this unsupported-layout policy.
- Wrong descriptor coordinates, reversed winding, moved/rebuilt candidate mesh,
  and malformed geometry that contradicts testable proof are `FAIL`.
- These checks are run after current checkpoint artifact byte/SHA/size validation
  and canonical solver replay, but before the current realized GLB world-frame
  and triangle contact/support checks. Direct QA calls are likewise scoped to
  the exact GLB supplied at that invocation.
- `geometryDigest` and `surfaceDigest` remain useful typed bookkeeping.
  Neither is independently accepted as proof of realized geometry; actual GLB
  triangle content is compared. Correct correspondence of a physical mesh's
  part **meaning** to the photographed object remains QA-03 #271, not this
  geometry-only verifier.

Evidence scope is static, unskinned, FLOAT32 triangle meshes with currently
supported accessor layouts. This does not attest unseen back surfaces, primary
source likeness, every candidate mesh triangle (descriptors are selected
patches), complex fusion-frame aliasing, intentional split shell support or
Game Ready claims. A positive with an actual GLB plus changed-GLB and
descriptor-drift negatives must pass before treating this sub-slice as closed.

## QA-02j — independently replayed native fusion aliases vs GLB world poses

For a propagation-bound assembly, one welded physical GLB node may represent
several declared semantic FUSED members. The old pose checker required a mesh
node for **every** semantic entity, so correct native fusion could not satisfy
the source-bound propagation proof. Simply replacing missing IDs with an alias
map would be a self-certification bypass.

Now the pose checker accepts fusion aliases **only** after replaying the
existing trusted `replayExactGlbPhysicalFusion` authority against the exact
current GLB, original pre-fusion GLB, signed pre-fusion checkpoint, typed native
WELD_SHARED_BOUNDARY plan, canonical edit intent, logical fusion, and persisted
report/provenance. Each alias set must equal the native bake's complete member
inventory and the contact plan's digest-bound fusion binding. Missing/duplicate
evidence stays INSUFFICIENT; incompatible or forged members/provenance fail.
The public pose API's fusion-aware option runs the trusted replay itself;
caller-supplied alias maps are never treated as standalone proof.

At the QA-02j baseline, all semantic member frames had to agree
with the physical root and only identity, unparented single-primitive
native meshes were admitted. QA-02n subsequently established actual
multi-primitive/parented rigid GLB replay; QA-02o below requires the
original pre-fusion member's independently realized GLB frame to match
its corresponding propagated semantic pose. No synthetic member frame
offset based solely on final physical root coordinates is authorized.
Unfused physical nodes and mismatched GLB poses remain blocked as before.

This work is a geometry/kinematics-consistency gate, not independent
source-photo matching. Complex multi-root fusion, skinned assemblies, generated
solid-union backends, and source-observed part correctness remain unclosed.

## QA-02k — source/candidate-bound exact opposed-face component support

A physical GLB node with multiple **geometrically disconnected** triangle
islands is not necessarily a floating component: UV/material seams are already
coalesced using exact oppositely wound geometric edges in the existing
`triangle-component-inventory`. A deliberately separate pair of closed shells
can also share a flush face without an index-edge weld. Neither a worker's
label nor a whole-node support-root declaration is enough to accept it.

This first bounded component-contact slice introduces a distinct,
checkpoint-SHA-bound `triangle-component-support-plan` artifact with the
current GLB SHA, unchanged inventory digest and precisely one physical-root
triangle plus explicit directed child/owner triangle witnesses for each
geometric island. The existing inventory routine remains the source of exact
Float32 triangle coordinates and component identity; exposing a trusted
in-memory view **does not change any persisted inventory payload/digest**.

The trusted assembly QA recomputes, from current GLB bytes, that each
declared child/owner face pair belongs to two different geometric islands,
has the **same three exact Float32 vertex positions with opposing winding**
and positive area, and that every island has exactly one witness path back
to the declared root triangle. Missing witnesses and unresolved
nonmanifold/ambiguous overlaps remain INSUFFICIENT; fabricated face
contacts, invalid triangle IDs, stale asset/inventory/digests and modified
checkpoint JSON are FAIL. A sufficiently small but nonzero physical gap
can **never** be reclassified as support using a distance threshold.

The public candidate-only API and checkpoint-bound QA share the same replay;
no alternative contact certificate is introduced. Existing contact/support
pair replay and semantic coverage remain mandatory.

**Scope boundary:** a successful exact-face contact graph demonstrates only
source/candidate-bound **geometric face contact**, not welded material,
load-bearing dynamics, or semantic correspondence to a photo. Noncoincident
coplanar patches, edge contacts, angled woven joints, joints that need
mechanical fasteners, intentionally free/floating components and true
interpenetrating connectors require future specialized, independently
admitted proofs; neither a rationale-only declaration nor arbitrary
`NOT_APPLICABLE` can waive the rule. The independent QA-03 primary-source
semantic gate is not yet closed, so this PASS cannot certify visual
resemblance or Game Ready quality.

## QA-02l — sparse GLB geometry authority, narrow static surface/triangle replay

A GLB 2.0 accessor can contain an optional sparse override on a dense array,
or omit `bufferView` and start from an implicit zero array. The earlier QA
readers either rejected sparse geometry or used different dense readers. A
worker could not obtain a trusted source-bound geometry result for a legitimate
sparse mesh, and downstream checks lacked one common sparse-byte contract.

The trusted `qa-glb-geometry-accessors.mjs` reader now materializes both
supported formats using the **actual candidate GLB BIN bytes**, not
`min/max` or worker-authored digest metadata. Supported fields are static
FLOAT32 `VEC3` POSITION and unsigned `SCALAR` triangle indices
(`UNSIGNED_BYTE`, `UNSIGNED_SHORT`, `UNSIGNED_INT`) with optional
sparse overlays. Sparse override indices use their own unsigned component
type, must be strictly increasing, unique, and in range; sparse values may
not overrun their bufferView. Base accessors without a bufferView default
to zero. Every dense/sparse bufferView is checked against the *declared*
embedded GLB byte length, type, stride, component alignment and local offset.

The same decoder is used by the existing authoritative:
- triangle-component inventory and current GLB rooted component support;
- actual triangle-level contact/support narrow phase; and
- owner-local surface-descriptor witness replay.

Existing contact plans, GLB SHA checks, typed source/checkpoint binding,
and geometric inventory contents remain under their own independent replay.
Valid sparse-only and dense-with-sparse-patch triangles must produce real
PASS when appropriate; modified positions and invalid sparse offsets, duplicate,
unordered or out-of-range override indices produce FAIL, never fabricated PASS.

**Limit:** this is static GLB triangle-geometry decoding, not arbitrary
attribute, GPU quantization, skinned/morphed geometry, verified source-photo
likeness or physically load-bearing simulation. The special exact native
physical fusion replay refused sparse input/final layouts at QA-02l;
QA-02m below adds narrowly replayed native sparse geometry equivalence.
Unrelated multi-primitive, morph/skinning and frame-offset layouts still
require separate independent authority. Independently observed reference semantics remain QA-03 #271.

## QA-02m — exact sparse native fusion from pre-fusion and final GLB

The original strict native `WELD_SHARED_BOUNDARY` replay decoded GLB
POSITION and indices with a standalone dense-only reader. QA-02l made
the triangle inventory, contact and surface patch replay sparse-aware, but
physical fusion still refused legal sparse-only or dense+sparse layouts.

The existing **same trusted native fusion replay** now consumes precisely
the QA-02l bounded decoder for static, indexed FLOAT32 VEC3 POSITION and
unsigned SCALAR triangle indices. It materializes sparse overrides from
the real pre-fusion and final GLB bytes, checks the original pre-fusion SHA
and checkpoint lineage, recreates the canonical native WELD bake,
revalidates the fusion report/provenance, and compares the current final
sparse geometry to the reproduced fused triangle soup and winding in
native member order. GLB extras must bind the exact report/provenance.
An equivalent sparse representation is allowed without treating metadata
or a GLB digest alone as a geometry certificate.

Regression includes actual cube-based head/face/nose native fusion,
both pre-fusion GLB and result GLB converted to **sparse-only POSITION
and indices with implicit-zero dense base**, dense and sparse equivalent
output, mutated sparse position, duplicate/unordered sparse override
indices, and stale pre-fusion SHA. Source-bound contact replay with
the real native fusion inputs and semantic alias mapping is also required.

At QA-02m, native replay still required indexed TRIANGLES with one
primitive and identity unparented rigid frames. QA-02n below extends this
narrowly to multi-primitive indexed meshes and bound rigid parent frames,
without authorizing skinning, morphs, nonrigid or source-photo semantics. Multi-primitive native fusion,
SOLID_UNION backend evidence, deformed poses, partial mechanical
interlocks and physically load-bearing support are not certified here.

## QA-02n — real GLB multi-primitive and rigid-parent native fusion replay

Original and fused `WELD_SHARED_BOUNDARY` meshes may be split into several
indexed TRIANGLES primitives (e.g. GLB material partitions) while preserving
a common POSITION accessor and ordered face stream. The previously trusted
fusion replay refused every such layout and any node with a transform,
despite the native fusion baker itself supporting attested member world frames.

The native replay now uses one shared active GLB scene/rigid-frame parser
(the same evaluator used by attachment propagation QA). It independently
traverses active scene nodes and their parent chains, checks unique physical
part identities, and rejects uninstantiated, cyclic, multiply-parented,
non-rigid, scaled, sheared or reflected geometry. Each active member's exact
world frame is bound by `physicalFusionFrameDigest` to the authoritative
fusion plan, not inferred from the fusion output or a claimed alias map.
The final physical root's GLB world frame must agree with the plan's
`fusionRootFrame`.

For each physical input/fused mesh, indexed TRIANGLES primitives are
deterministically flattened **in primitive order**; a repeated POSITION
accessor shares one vertex pool and an independently numbered POSITION
accessor introduces a new ordered pool. Index streams retain their winding
and offsets. All underlying sparse or dense GLB accessor bytes are decoded
via QA-02l, and both original/checkpoint SHA and final native geometry
equality remain independently required. Exact native bake
report/provenance and GLB extras are unchanged.

Regression: three real source members in a rigidly translated GLB parent
tree, each subdivided into two indexed primitives; fused output split into
two primitives and translated into the same actual root world frame. Native
rebake and contact/support fusion alias must pass. Changed output world
pose, ungrounded original candidate, duplicate primitive indices and
unindexed primitives cannot produce PASS; non-rigid scale remains
INSUFFICIENT, not synthetic geometry.

**Limits at QA-02n:** physically differently transformed original
members could be rebuilt, but the source-bound propagation alias gate
still rejected differing semantic member poses. QA-02o below closes
that exact contract by independently proving each original GLB member
world frame from the native bake before accepting an alias. Original source-photo part roles, material
partition semantics, complex SOLID_UNION and physics/load-bearing are
independent unfinished gates.

## QA-02o — exact native original-GLB member-world-frame provenance for FUSED propagation

Prior QA-02j correctly rejected semantic FUSED entities claiming a GLB
root alias without an independently replayed physical fusion bake, but it
also treated **every** post-fusion semantic member's propagated pose as
identical to the final fused GLB physical root. This disallowed legitimate
parts that originally had their own rigid translation/rotation and were
subsequently physically baked into the root. QA-02n independently bound
each pre-fusion GLB member's actual world frame to the native bake, but
the propagated semantic alias check still lacked that information.

The existing `replayExactGlbPhysicalFusion` success now exposes a
read-only `memberWorldFrames` inventory derived exclusively from the
real source/checkpoint-bound original GLB's active scene rigid transforms.
No member frame is returned on FAIL/INSUFFICIENT. The replay retains all
requirements: exact original GLB SHA, signed pre-fusion checkpoint, exact
plan member geometry/frame digests, independently recomputed native
weld, actual fused GLB geometry and physical root world frame, and
report/provenance on the actual GLB.

The fusion-aware `verifyRealizedPropagationWorldFrames` admits each
**non-root FUSED** semantic member only when its independently replayed
propagation world pose matches that member's original GLB world frame.
The **physical fusion root** must instead match the actual final
GLB root world frame. All alias IDs must cover the exact physical fusion
member list and no duplicate original/final node is permitted; aliases
cannot be used for non-FUSED semantic relations. Missing or
incomplete independently realized fusion evidence is INSUFFICIENT,
while forged semantic poses, stale original GLB, frame mismatch,
incorrect native provenance, and post-fusion root pose mismatch FAIL.

Regression demonstrates a three-piece head with an original face
locally rotated 180 degrees and translated and a separately moved nose;
their local mesh coordinates are countertransformed so the trusted
physical native bake produces the same single continuous head shell.
The source-bound propagation plan and contact witness PASS only with
the exact original per-member poses; copying the nose frame to the
face or altering the saved original geometry/pose does not pass.

**Provenance boundary:** this proves a part's original pre-fusion GLB
world-space placement was preserved in the physical native bake and
matches source-bound semantic propagation. It does not prove the
source photo contains those parts, or that the original interpretation
of their shapes or purpose was correct. QA-03 #271, early whole-image
camera/shape #272, mechanical nonexact structural support and final
QA-01/P2 #265 remain independent, unclosed requirements.
