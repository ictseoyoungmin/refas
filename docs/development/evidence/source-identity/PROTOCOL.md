## What the visual work must resolve

The jacket needs a readable turned collar and notched lapels, plausible tailored
cloth, dark straps separated from bright buckles/edging, and coherent left-sided
shield/chain/pendant identity. Its continuous armholes, cropped/open body and
local cuff edit remain usable.

The arm needs source-shaped nested armor, recesses and exposed mechanics, a
readable service assembly, and a differentiated contoured palm/five-digit hand.
Original rigid pivots and local cover movement must remain usable. New parts
must follow their intended moving parent.

Use the same actual cameras, lights and renderer for before/after pairs. Any
additional close view must be rendered for both states. Source-sheet cameras
remain unregistered; do not infer calibrated likeness or dimensional error from
image overlap. Inspect complete source, whole renders and relevant details.
Retain rejected iterations and the reasons for subsequent corrections.
