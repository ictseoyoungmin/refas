(roughly 703 MiB), including JIT and other resident allocations.

this particular observed high-water. The candidate subsequently raised
its fixed JIT/native headroom by 192 MiB to avoid overstating
memory-budget acceptance. This needs further cross-host measurement;
the memory estimate is NOT a hard OS memory limiter.
